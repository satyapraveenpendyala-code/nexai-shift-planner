# SOP-GEN: General machine inspection

## 1. When this applies
Use this procedure when the failure mode is unclear or no specific procedure fits. It covers a basic safe inspection and when to escalate.

## 2. Safety
Stop the machine and apply lockout/tagout before any inspection. Never bypass guards or interlocks.

## 3. Inspection
Record the machine's temperatures, speed, torque and tool wear. Look for unusual noise, vibration, heat, leaks or damage.

## 4. Escalation
If the cause is not clear or the fix is outside your skill, keep the machine locked out and escalate to the shift supervisor. Record what you checked and what you found.
