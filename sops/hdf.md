# SOP-HDF: Heat dissipation failure

## 1. When this applies
Use this procedure when the predicted failure mode is heat dissipation. In the AI4I data, it occurs when the process temperature minus the air temperature is below 8.6 K and rotational speed is below 1380 rpm.

## 2. Safety
Stop the machine and apply lockout/tagout before opening any cooling or electrical covers. Let hot surfaces cool before touching them.

## 3. Inspection
Read the air and process temperatures and compute the temperature difference, and note the rotational speed. Check the cooling fans, air flow paths and heat exchanger for blockage, dust or damage.

## 4. Corrective action
Clean blocked air flow paths and the heat exchanger, and restore any failed fan or cooling flow to correct the heat dissipation problem. If the temp difference stays below 8.6 K after cleaning, escalate to the shift supervisor.

## 5. Restart check
Remove lockout/tagout only after covers are refitted and the area is clear. Run the machine and confirm the temperature difference rises above 8.6 K or speed stays above 1380 rpm.
